Who we are

Built out of necessity

This app was built by a finance student who couldn't find a free, private, European-friendly portfolio tracker. No ads, no account required, no data ever leaves your device. Everything runs in your browser.

Transparency

How data works

📡 Live Prices

Source: Yahoo Finance via a Cloudflare Worker proxy. Prices refresh every 5 minutes. Data may be delayed 15–20 minutes depending on the exchange.

📊 Historical Prices

Source: Yahoo Finance historical data (v8 API). Used for performance charts and backtest. Data goes back 20+ years for major indices. European ETFs may have shorter history.

💱 Exchange Rates

Source: Yahoo Finance FX rates (USD/CHF, EUR/CHF). Updated with each price refresh.

💾 Your Data

Your portfolio data is stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage. It is never uploaded, never shared, never seen by anyone. Use the Export function regularly as a backup.

A word on accuracy

Honest about precision

Yahoo Finance is accurate enough for portfolio tracking at any reasonable scale. Small discrepancies (0.1–0.5%) may exist due to FX timing or delayed prices. For portfolios under CHF 1,000,000 these differences are negligible. When the user base reaches 100+ users, the app will migrate to EOD Historical Data — a professional paid provider with full European exchange coverage. This upgrade will remain free for all users.
